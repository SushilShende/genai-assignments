2) Read 2–3 articles about how transformer works?
Goal to understand:
(a) Self-attention mechanism	
The self-attention mechanism is the core innovation behind modern Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT, Claude, and Gemini. Introduced in the landmark 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al., it allows a model to weigh the importance of different words in a sequence when processing each word.

(b) Vector embedding		
Vector embedding is the process of converting words, sentences, images, or any data into lists of numbers (vectors) that capture their meaning in a mathematical space.

(c) Feed forward Nueral Network	-
A Feed-Forward Neural Network is the simplest and most fundamental type of artificial neural network, where information flows in one direction only — from input → hidden layers → output — with no loops or cycles.

Core Idea is
Data enters the network, gets transformed through a series of layers using weights, biases, and activation functions, and produces an output. There is no feedback hence the name "feed-forward."
